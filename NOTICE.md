## Changes made

Adaptation © 2026 Guram Zhgamadze, distributed under the same licence (CC BY 4.0).

- The book's Georgian-language machine-readable specification was selected,
  condensed, reorganised and translated into English as Claude agent-skill
  files (`georgian-grammar/SKILL.md` and `georgian-grammar/references/*.md`).
- Examples that conflict with standard Georgian grammar were corrected. Each
  correction, with the literature that supports it, is listed in
  [`georgian-grammar/references/about-this-source.md`](georgian-grammar/references/about-this-source.md).
- Material from other published sources was added (case marking by tense
  series, reported-speech particles, the evidential perfect) and is credited
  where it appears.
- A section index for the book was added
  (`georgian-grammar/references/source-book-index.md`).
- The original publication is included **unmodified** as
  `georgian-grammar/references/source-book.pdf`;
  `georgian-grammar/references/source-book-fulltext.txt` is a plain-text
  extraction of that same PDF.
